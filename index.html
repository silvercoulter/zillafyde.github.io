About ROCK N' ROLL McDONALD'S

Biography

Zillafyde is a groove rock band hailing from Bloomington, Indiana. They perform a mix of original compositions and popular cover songs, featuring an eclectic mix of styles that occasionally captivate and sometimes just confuse audiences. Drawing inspiration from classic artists like Led Zeppelin and Jimi Hendrix, contemporary artists like The Revivalists, improvisational acts like Umphrey's McGee and Goose, and rock duo Local H. We've synthesized it all into something that's either genius or unlistenable—you decide.

History

In late summer 2022, a group of musicians got together at the weekly open mic held at the Cabin Restaurant and Lounge. Scott and Silver's chemistry from these experiences helped create the wall of sound that eventually became Zillafyde.

The Band

Scott See

Frontman & Guitarist - Started playing guitar in 2002. Primary songwriter for the band. The guy who yells into the microphone while the rest of us try not to laugh.

Silver Coulter

Co-founder & Lead Guitarist - Over 20 years of guitar experience. Influences include Led Zeppelin, Brad Paisley, Local H, and Tool. Secretly checks the setlist three times before every show.

Joey Carpenter

Drums - Playing in bands since high school. Influences from Tool, Tower of Power, and Led Zeppelin. Hits things harder than anyone has a right to.

Brady Mikels

Bass & Guitar - From Bedford, Indiana. Playing since age 10. Inspired by Led Zeppelin, Gary Clark Jr., The Doors, and Marcus King. The quiet one until the bass drops.
